Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. TrackOR has no account, no server and no cloud sync.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings → Export data and save the CSV file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example iCloud Drive via Files). On the new phone, go to Settings → Import data and pick that file. Importing replaces everything currently in the app on that phone.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for TrackOR in your device's Settings, and that the reminder is switched on in the app's Schedule tab. Morning reminders only fire on the days you've marked as planned office days. End-of-day reminders only fire on working days you haven't logged yet.

How are the percentages calculated?

See Settings → Help & calculation rules in the app for the full rules.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all data on this device, or delete the app.
