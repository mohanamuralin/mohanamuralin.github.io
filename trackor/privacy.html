Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

TrackOR is a personal tool for logging where you worked each day (office, work from home, leave or public holiday) and tracking that against your own office-attendance target. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

The short version

TrackOR does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

TrackOR stores the following information locally, in a database on your device:

  • Profile names and settings you create (for example week-off days, office target percentage and how leave is counted).
  • Your daily log entries (date and work status).
  • App preferences (theme, display options) and reminder settings.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. The developer cannot see or access it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, TrackOR asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. No push-notification service or server is used. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device's Settings at any time.

Export and import

You can export your data as a CSV file from Settings. The file is handed to the system share sheet, and you decide where it goes (for example Files, email or another app). Once exported, that copy is handled by whichever app or service you choose, under its own privacy policy. Importing a file reads it only to replace the data on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time from Settings → Delete all data on this device. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

TrackOR does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
